# Bingham Canyon Mine - design blueprint

Status: **built from public data, static gates green** (2026-09-15). Not yet play-tested.

## The site

Kennecott's open pit south-west of Salt Lake City: an inverted mountain 4 km wide and
1.2 km deep, terraced in 15 m benches and threaded by continuous 8-10 % haul roads
that spiral from the rim to the floor. The 2013 Manefay landslide re-shaped the
north-east wall; the 2023 lidar has the post-slide surface. Around the pit, graded
waste-rock terraces and the Oquirrh foothills.

## The dataset

- **USGS 3DEP 1 m** from the UT 2023 Salt Lake County lidar.
- **USGS NAIP** at 1.5 m; **OpenStreetMap** has the haul and access road network
  mapped as service roads, which the pack drapes as 151 decal roads (103 km).

## Why the physics pops

Civil earthwork is the cleanest geometry a lidar survey can return: crisp bench
edges, uniform grades and long sight lines. That makes it a brake-fade and sustained
engine-load bench for heavy haulers, and a missed corner is a multi-stage crash across
dozens of 15 m benches, every one of them measured.

## Driving profile

Loaded T-Series, belly dumps and mixers on the haul roads; descents from the rim
under sustained braking; the pit floor for recovery; the waste-rock terraces for
open free-roam.

## Level decisions

| Decision | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Footprint | 6144 m, centred 40.523 N 112.151 W | The full pit, its rim and the terraces beyond |
| Samples | 4096 @ 1.5 m | Benches are 15 m tall and 30 m wide; 1.5 m keeps their edges |
| Materials | haul gravel (<7 deg), waste rock (14-32 deg), bench face (>32 deg), scrub hillside | Slope-painted |
| Spawns | pit rim east (default), pit floor, north terraces | Rim first, for the descent |
| Roads | 151 decal roads, 102.9 km | Every OSM service, track and unclassified way inside the footprint |
| Sky | 14:50 local, late June | High sun so every bench face is lit |
