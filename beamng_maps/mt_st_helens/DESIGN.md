# Mount St. Helens Pumice Plain - design blueprint

Status: **built from public data, static gates green** (2026-09-15). Not yet play-tested.

## The site

The amphitheater the 1980 eruption left: a horseshoe crater open to the north with a
600 m headwall, the dacite lava dome growing on its floor, and the Pumice Plain
running north from the crater mouth toward Spirit Lake. Glacial melt and rain have cut
braided, sheer-walled canyon networks through the ash and pumice, and the debris
avalanche left boulder-strewn hummocks along the North Fork Toutle River.

## The dataset

- **USGS 3DEP 1 m** from the WA FEMAHQ 2018 lidar, which post-dates the 2004-2008
  dome-building eruptions, so the dome and the Crater Glacier are in the surface.
- **USGS NAIP** at 1.5 m; **OpenStreetMap** has only trails here, so one short track
  ships and the washes are the roads.

## Why the physics pops

Scale. The level is the largest in the pack (6144 m at 1.5 m per sample) because the
amphitheater needs all of it: 1525 m of relief from the plain to the crater rim. The
ash canyons keep their vertical walls at 1.5 m spacing (they are 10-30 m deep), and the
hummock field reads as real 5-20 m mounds rather than noise.

## Driving profile

Deep technical wash navigation, crawling across the hummocks and the dome's debris
apron, and vertical drop-offs off the canyon rims. Snow and ice paint the steep upper
crater walls.

| Footprint | 6144 m, centred 46.222 N 122.190 W | Crater rim at the south edge, hummocks and Spirit Lake shore at the north |
| Samples | 4096 @ 1.5 m | The only way to hold the amphitheater in one terrain block |
| Materials | pumice plain, ash gully (5-16 deg), debris slope (16-34 deg), crater wall (>34 deg), snow/ice (upper 14 %, >20 deg) | Slope + elevation painted |
| Spawns | Pumice Plain (default), crater mouth, Toutle hummocks | Start on the plain and choose up or down |
| Roads | 1 decal road, 0.3 km | Honest: there are none |
| Sky | 09:36 local, August | Morning light into the north-facing crater |
