## 🔌 WIRING DOUBLE-CHECK

HLK-LD2450        →  ESP32-S3 ProS3
────────────────     ─────────────────
TX (Yellow wire)  →  IO44 (RX pin)
RX (Green wire)   →  IO43 (TX pin)
VCC (Red wire)    →  5V
GND (Black wire)  →  GND

### Common Mistakes:
❌ TX/RX swapped (most common!)
❌ Using 3.3V instead of 5V
❌ GND not connected
❌ Wrong GPIO pins
